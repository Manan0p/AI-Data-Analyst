## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an ultra-minimalist, Scandinavian-inspired, and highly rational workspace engineered for autonomous multi-agent data intelligence. The experience targets quantitative researchers, analytics engineers, and executive decision-makers who require deep analytical rigor without visual chaos.

The aesthetic merges clinical precision with natural airiness: spacious compositions, high typographic legibility, micro-elevations, and intentional data-density primitives. Rather than overwhelming the user with heavy borders or opaque panels, the system relies on delicate white planes, muted slate foundations, and hyper-targeted semantic accents to focus human attention directly onto agent workflows, streaming data vectors, and synthesis narratives.
